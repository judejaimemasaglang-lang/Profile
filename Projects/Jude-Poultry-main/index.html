Our Story

Golden Comb Poultry was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves access to clean, healthy, ethically raised chicken. Our birds roam freely on open pasture, fed a balanced natural diet with no antibiotics.

We categorize our chickens by age so you always know exactly what you're getting — tender young birds for grilling, balanced 5-month birds for roasting, and mature birds for rich, flavorful stews.

From our family farm to your kitchen, we're committed to quality, transparency, and care.
